About Me

Dr. Sarah Calloway, LCSW

Licensed Clinical Social Worker EMDR Certified CBT Specialist

I believe therapy is one of the most courageous things a person can do. My approach is warm, collaborative, and deeply rooted in respect for each person's unique story. I don't believe in a one-size-fits-all model — together, we'll find what works for you.

With over a decade of experience working with adults navigating anxiety, depression, relationship challenges, and the aftermath of trauma, I draw from evidence-based practices including Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR), and mindfulness-based interventions.

I completed my MSW at Columbia University and hold my doctorate from NYU. I am fully licensed in New York and New Jersey, and offer both in-person sessions in Manhattan and secure telehealth appointments nationwide.

Getting Started

Your Path to Healing

Beginning therapy can feel daunting. Here's exactly what to expect when you reach out.

01

Reach Out

Complete the contact form or call the office. I personally respond to all inquiries within one business day. There's no pressure — this is simply a conversation.

02

Free 15-Min Consultation

We'll have a brief phone call so you can share what's bringing you to therapy and I can answer any questions. This helps us both make sure we're a good fit before committing.

03

Begin Your Journey

We schedule your first full session — 50 minutes — and begin the real work together. Most clients start to notice meaningful shifts within the first few months.
